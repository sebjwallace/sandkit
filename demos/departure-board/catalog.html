The scenario catalog

The board only ever sees four facts about a train, so every situation it can meet is one combination of them: 108 in all. Each is a scenario, with its own code: a train's line on the board, the details it came from, and given its facts, then what each part of the board shows. Scenarios the rules treat the same way, through the same rules, form a family.

A scenario is seen when a train in the chosen set has those facts, and shown with that train; given when someone gave an example for it on the By example page; otherwise only inferred, shown with a made-up train that has those facts.

Try: search for Oxford to find its scenario, SC-021, or choose seen to see only what real trains do. Then choose Delay: unknown and sort by Largest first. Then change a rule on the rules page and come back: the codes stay, and the cards move to their new families.
