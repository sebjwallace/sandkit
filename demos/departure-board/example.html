Goal: design what a station's departure board shows by giving examples of what it should show, and letting the rules follow.

Programming by example

Rather than writing rules, say what one part of the board should show for one train. The model finds a rule that makes it so: as wide as it can be without changing anything you haven't asked for, placed just above the rule that decides that train now. Every example is kept, and checked against the rules from then on, here and on the rules page.

Or don't say it at all: on Choose a line, pick from variations of the train's whole line, as in selective breeding, generation after generation, and use the one you like.

Try: click Brighton on the board, choose the Expected column, and make it show Boarding. The model proposes "running and due now". Switch off Leave the other trains as they are to see how far one example alone would reach, then switch it back and add the rule.

Examples so far

The trains as the board receives them. Change anything, add or remove trains, and the board follows.
