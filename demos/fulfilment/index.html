Goal: understand how a spare-parts warehouse decides which depot's order gets scarce stock, and when it leaves, by watching its model grow one idea at a time.

How the warehouse came to be

A warehouse supplies rail maintenance depots with parts. Its model is a lineage of small versions, each one runnable, each adding one idea to its parent. A version runs a whole day: every hour on the hour, deliveries land, waiting order lines take stock, and orders decide whether they can go.

Try: pick V2 and scrub through the day: who is left without couplers at 09:00? Then pick the rival V2b at the same hour and see who loses instead. In V4, watch which orders make the 16:00 van. In V10, watch the ledger at 13:00, then pick V11.
