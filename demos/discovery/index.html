Claims and their sources

Usually called sensemaking, or evidence synthesis. Everything here is source material, the agent's own analyses included, and each passage that speaks to a claim is an assertion: yes or no. A claim is a group of assertions that say the same thing, under a line someone wrote for them. Its standing is worked out from its assertions, never labelled by hand.

Questions and decisions have cards of their own, since each does a different job: a question gathers the passages that answer it, and a decision records what was chosen and why.

Try: click a passage or a source to open the source in full. Point at a passage to find its source below, or at a source to light every passage it gives.

How it connects

One iteration of the loop, on three boards, each laid out by the part a card plays. What we know: the investigations that gathered sources, the values captured from them, what was interpreted, reasoned and assumed, what is still open, and what is still being looked into. Where we’re going: what was in force, what was decided, and what that brought about. What we’re building: entities and domain primitives proposed for the model, integrated into it, or rejected, and the scenarios and rules that test it, passing or failing as they run against the model; each is founded on cards from the other boards, and what an integrated part is comes from its class in the model’s code. No card is ever edited: later cards test or supersede it, and what follows is worked out for each iteration. Point at a card to see what it rests on and what rests on it; click to open it. Click a column’s title to fold it down to its codes.

Sources

    Scope: what a source can speak to

    Role
    Which kind of claim. A record speaks to what is or was, a plan to what should be, a decision to what was chosen, an opinion to what people think.
    As of
    Which moment it describes. An old source isn't worse; it speaks to an earlier time.

    Grade: how well it speaks

    Distance
    How far from what happened: direct, first-hand or second-hand.
    Trust
    Whether whoever made it is able and willing to get it right: trusted, unknown or doubtful.
    Clarity
    How well the record kept it: clean, partial or noisy.

    Standing: worked out from assertions

    corroborated
    Two or more independent sources for, none against.
    single source
    One independent source for, none against.
    contested
    Sources for and against.
    refuted
    Only sources against.
    unbacked
    Nothing that speaks to the world backs it: a conjecture, or an assumption once something rests on it.

    What we know

    Investigation
    Finding what’s already there; it gathers sources.
    Source
    Material from the world, never edited.
    Observation
    A value captured from a source.
    Metric
    A quantity tracked over time; observations are its readings.
    Claim
    Something said about the world, weighed on its sources.
    Definition
    What a word means here.
    Opinion
    What someone thinks should be, and why.
    Inference
    Reasoned from other cards; as sound as its premises.
    Assumption
    Held without evidence; tested by later cards.
    Question
    Something open.

    Where we’re going

    Outcome
    What happened, measured against a goal or a prediction.
    Goal
    A target on a metric.
    Prediction
    A decision’s bet on what it will bring about.
    Agreement
    Terms between parties, changed only with them.
    Constraint
    A limit to work within: where it comes from, what it protects.
    Decision
    What was chosen, and why.
    Experiment
    A test run on purpose, on the world or on the model.
    Action
    Something that has to happen.
    Risk
    Something that could go wrong.

    What we’re building

    Entity
    A thing in the domain with properties and behaviour; a class in the model.
    Domain primitive
    A value with constraints and no behaviour, such as a delay.
    Scenario
    Given, When, Then, played against the model.
    Rule
    A mandate or conditional, checked against the model.
