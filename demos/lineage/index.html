Goal: understand a station's departure board by watching its model grow, from the simplest board to the full picture, one idea at a time.

How the board came to be

Every domain starts small and simple and grows step by step. Here the departure board's model is a lineage of small versions, each one runnable, each adding one idea to its parent, with a note on what caused it. Rivals that were tried and set aside stay, as dead ends.

Try: step along the trunk and watch the same twelve trains gain detail. Lines marked in amber look different from the version before, with what they showed then. At V4, look at Dover Priory: what's wrong with it, and how does V5 fix it?

Each fact this version knows, then each part of the board, with every value once. A train's facts and what the board shows for it make a pathway across. Pathways that share a rule form a bundle, which fans out across every value of a fact the rule doesn't care about. Point at a tile, or at a train on the board, to light its pathways.
