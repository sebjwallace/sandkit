## 0.1.0 · 2026-10-22
- First release. Inspect mode, chosen in the shell's mode picker: pointing outlines the innermost part the sandbox can `explain` and, after a moment, shows what it is in a card at the side; a click holds the card on it instead of acting. The card is dragged by its bar. Pointing at nothing keeps what's shown. A banner says what pointing does, with Stop; Escape ends the mode when no dialog is open. Clicks on `data-modes` and in open dialogs pass through. Amber, like every mode.
