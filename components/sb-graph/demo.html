Things joined by connections, with what's happening now lit up.

About

Many systems are best understood as things and the connections between them: the states a machine can be in and the moves between them, the stages of a workflow, the places goods or water or messages flow between. sb-graph draws them, and lights up what is happening now: the current state, the connection about to be taken, even the single rule on that connection that applies. Watching the highlight move is often the moment a system clicks.

Where it helps

  • State machines. Turnstiles, traffic lights, Turing machines, protocols, an order's lifecycle.
  • Flows and networks. Supply chains, water and power networks, services passing messages, with things moving along the connections.
  • Workflows. Who passes what to whom, and where things wait.

Where something else fits better

  • Large graphs. Dozens of nodes become a tangle. Show the part that matters, or split it across chapters.
  • Graphs that need automatic layout. This version places nodes in a circle or where you put them. Hand-placed positions read best for anything beyond a handful of nodes.
  • Quantities over time. A chart says more about how a level changes than a meter does.

Getting started

<script src="components/sb-graph/sb-graph.js"></script>
<sb-graph></sb-graph>

<script>
  const graph = document.querySelector('sb-graph');
  graph.data = {
    nodes: [{ id: 'locked', label: 'Locked', shape: 'circle' }, { id: 'open', label: 'Unlocked', shape: 'circle' }],
    edges: [{ from: 'locked', to: 'open', label: 'coin' }, { from: 'open', to: 'locked', label: 'push' }]
  };
  graph.active = { nodes: ['locked'] };
</script>

Making it reveal, not just diagram

  • Keep active in step with the model. Highlight the current state and the transition just taken on every step.
  • Give rule lines ids. When several rules share a connection, highlighting the one that applied shows exactly why the model moved.
  • Use tones for trouble only. A warning or a failure should stand out against calm nodes.
