## 0.1.0 · 2026-10-19
- First release. A sandbox's source material as cards of paper, each with its format, summary and labelled author, date written and status, what it supersedes or is superseded by, and what it was derived from. Search; filters for author, format, stance and status, each value with how many sources it would show; a slider choosing the dates written between; primary only; sorting by date, title or author. Files in the sources folder not yet catalogued are listed after the cards. Opening, pointing, `selected` and `highlight`.
