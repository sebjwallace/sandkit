Scrub back and forth through anything with a beginning and an end.

About

Some ideas are about change from one state to another: a shape turning, a season passing, a ball rising and falling. Watching the change once helps; being able to stop it, rewind it and look closely at any moment helps far more. sb-scrubber gives a sandbox a scrubbable stretch of progress, with play and pause, and marks for the moments worth stopping at.

Where it helps

  • Transformations, from before to after, at any point in between.
  • Cycles, such as a year, a day or an orbit, looping round.
  • Processes with stages, where the marks name each stage.

Where something else fits better

  • Models that run without end. A simulation that steps forward forever needs play, pause and step, not a bounded range.
  • Choosing a setting. If the value is a parameter of the model rather than a moment in time, a parameter slider says so more clearly.

Getting started

<script src="components/sb-scrubber/sb-scrubber.js"></script>
<sb-scrubber min="0" max="1" duration="2"></sb-scrubber>

<script>
  const scrubber = document.querySelector('sb-scrubber');
  scrubber.marks = [{ value: 0, label: 'before' }, { value: 1, label: 'after' }];
  scrubber.addEventListener('sb-change', (e) => draw(e.detail.value));
</script>

Making it reveal, not just animate

  • Mark the moments that matter. The highest point, the halfway turn, the solstice: the marks tell people where to look.
  • Say where you are in words, through format, as well as by the slider's position.
  • Choose a duration that gives time to see. Too fast and it's a blur; slow enough, and people notice things.
