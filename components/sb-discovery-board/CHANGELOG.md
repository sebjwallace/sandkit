# sb-discovery-board changelog

## 0.1.0 · 2026-10-25
- First release. The sandbox's own items in columns by the part they play, placed by `data-ref`, running across or down; columns that fold, remembered by title; pointing or focus lights an item's chain back and forward and draws only those links: square routes with rounded corners, in lanes clear of the items they pass, labelled where they arrive without crowding; links in the theme's tones; `spacing` for the room between columns or bands; `highlight` and `reveal` from outside.
