Handing something to the agent as one precise prompt, and showing what comes back.

About

Sandboxes are built and changed by an AI agent, and people exploring one often want to ask it something from the page itself: why a figure is what it is, to try a new scenario, to check a reading, to add what they've just learned. The hand-off turns what's on the page into one precise prompt, shows it, says when it's been sent, and shows the reply when it comes back. Every round trip with the agent looks the same, so people learn it once.

Where it helps

  • A question the model can't answer, sent with the codes and state it's about.
  • Asking for a change: a new scenario, a rule corrected, a figure updated, with what prompted it.
  • Checking something doubtful, such as readings or citations people have marked.
  • Sending results on, such as answers to a poll, for the agent to make sense of.

Where something else fits better

  • Comments on anything on the page. Comment mode gathers many comments into one prompt.
  • A conversation. The hand-off is one round trip; talk to the agent directly for a discussion.

Getting started

<script src="components/sb-handoff/sb-handoff.js"></script>
<sb-handoff heading="Ask the agent"></sb-handoff>

<script>
  handoff.prompt = () => `# Question\n\n${question.value}\n\nWrite your reply into replies.js under QST-1.`;
  if (REPLIES['QST-1']) handoff.reply = REPLIES['QST-1'];   // the agent's reply, loaded as a script
</script>
  1. People press Copy as prompt; the prompt shows on the page and goes to the clipboard.
  2. They paste it to the agent, which writes its reply as data, where the prompt says.
  3. The page loads the reply and sets reply.

In the examples below, a button stands in for the agent writing its reply.

Making it reveal, not just display

  • Make the prompt precise: codes, the model's version, what's on screen, and where the reply goes.
  • Disable it until there's something to send, with waiting-text saying what's missing.
  • Show the reply where it belongs, beside what it answers, as well as in the hand-off.
