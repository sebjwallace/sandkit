# sb-tour changelog

## 0.1.1 · 2026-10-20
- A step scrolls the page to its part when the part isn't in full view.
- The spotlight and tooltip follow the part as it moves or changes size, not only on scrolling and resizing.

## 0.1.0 · 2026-10-18
- First release. A tour of the page as it stands: steps as HTML naming what they point at (`data-at`), a spotlight and a tooltip that follows the part, steps that wait for the viewer, a note when a step's part can't be found, the keyboard, and remembering per viewer whether it was finished.
