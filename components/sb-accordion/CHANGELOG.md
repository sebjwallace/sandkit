# sb-accordion changelog

## 0.1.0 · 2026-10-21
- First release. Sections that open and close, one on its own or a group: each a `<details>` child. A closed section shows a note from the model, a dot when its content has changed since last seen, and a tint when something highlighted is inside. Expand all and Collapse all for a group, or `single` for one open at a time; `reveal(ref, place)` and `sb-toggle`; accordions nest.
