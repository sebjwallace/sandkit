# sb-poll changelog

## 0.1.0 · 2026-10-20
- First release. A question put to a group: who has answered shows, never what, until everyone has and the sandbox reveals them, grouped by option with who chose each and why. An `outcome` makes it a prediction, marking what happened and who expected it. Options and people can be slotted with any content; notes show under each answer; options with a ref take part in highlighting; `describe()` gives the poll as text for a prompt.
