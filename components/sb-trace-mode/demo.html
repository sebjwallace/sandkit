Trace mode: click any part of a sandbox to see why it's there.

About

Everything in a good sandbox is there for a reason: a rule someone stated, a figure from a report, a design choice made after an interview. Trace mode lets anyone ask. Choose Trace in the header's mode picker, and every part that can be traced is outlined as you point at it; click one, and instead of doing what it normally does, the sandbox shows why it's there. Leave with Stop, Escape, or by choosing Explore again.

Where it helps

  • Sandboxes for decisions. When people will act on what a sandbox shows, they need to check what it rests on, part by part.
  • Reviewing a design. Click a button on a screen and see the requirement, and the conversation, behind it.
  • Spotting guesswork. A part resting on nothing cited shows up the moment someone asks.

Where something else fits better

  • Showing the trace itself. This only picks the part. Draw the trace itself in a modal, as the sources practice shows.
  • Commenting on a part. That's comment mode, the other mode in the picker.
  • Explaining one thing everyone should see. Write it beside the part; tracing is for whoever asks.

Getting started

<script src="components/sb-trace-mode/sb-trace-mode.js"></script>

<sb-shell>
  <sb-trace-mode slot="modes"></sb-trace-mode>
</sb-shell>

<script>
  const items = new Set(model.items.map((item) => item.id));
  trace.traceable = (ref) => items.has(ref);
  trace.addEventListener('sb-pick', (e) => openTrace(e.detail.ref));
</script>

Give every part worth asking about a data-ref with its code, and keep the codes in the model's items, each citing what it rests on, as the sources practice describes. It's a mode, so it shares the picker with commenting, one at a time: see the modes practice.

Making it reveal

  • Make everything that matters traceable, not just the obvious parts: a threshold, a label, a default.
  • Trace to the words. A trace that ends at a quote from a source is worth far more than one that ends at a document's name.
  • Let the gaps show. Leave parts that rest on nothing visibly so, rather than leaving them out of tracing.

The full contract, including every property, event and styling hook, is in the manifest.
