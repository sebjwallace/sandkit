Goal: see what parts of a page are about, and keep topics lit while you work. Press G to try it.
About

Where it helps

  • A sandbox that has opted in to tagging, with enough on the page that a topic is hard to follow by eye.
  • Preparing to talk about one topic: choose it, and see where it sits among everything else.
  • Finding where two topics meet, by choosing both.

Where something else fits

  • What one thing is, or why it's there: the other modes in the modes practice.
  • Everywhere one exact thing appears: its ref, through the highlighting practice. Tags are about topics.
  • A sandbox that hasn't opted in to tagging: there's nothing to show.

Making it reveal

  • Press G, point at a part below, and read what its tags mean.
  • Click a tag to keep it lit. Click a second: Intersection shows where they meet, Union shows both.
  • Press the arrow keys to visit what's lit. Press Esc: it stays lit, with a bar to clear it.
