# sb-annotate changelog

## 0.1.0 · 2026-10-17
- First release. Text written in plain HTML with the parts that matter wrapped in `<mark>`: pointing at a mark shows its note in a card just above it, and clicking pins the card open; kinds colour the marks, with a legend counting each that fades the other kinds when pointed at; marks with a `data-ref` are pointed at and highlighted.
