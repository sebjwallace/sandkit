## 0.1.0 · 2026-10-20
- First release. A decision as a card: context, options compared with their pros and cons, the one chosen marked and the rest kept in view, the reason, consequences, who decided and when, a status, and what it replaces or was replaced by. Missing parts are said plainly: warnings on an accepted decision, what's left to do on a proposal. Compact cards fold to the chosen option and the reason. With `editable`, a group records a decision in a workshop; the card fires the whole decision in `sb-save` and keeps nothing itself, and Reopen asks the sandbox for a new decision to replace an accepted one.
