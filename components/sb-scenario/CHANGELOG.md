# sb-scenario changelog

## 0.1.2 · 2026-10-25
- Manifest: names no other component, not even as a suggestion. Which components go together is for the practices.

## 0.1.1 · 2026-10-18
- Added `results`, from the model playing the scenario: each step marked as expected or not, with what actually happened where it differs, and the card saying whether the scenario played out as expected. Turning the model's knobs shows which outcome stops coming true and what came instead.

## 0.1.0 · 2026-10-18
- First release. A scenario as a card: its code and title, Given / When / Then steps with keywords lined up and And / But under the keyword they carry on, quoted text and numbers marked, highlighting of the card or single steps by ref, the current step marked with later ones faded, and selection.
