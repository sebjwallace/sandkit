# sb-lineage changelog

## 0.1.0 · 2026-10-27
- First release, grown from `sb-discovery-board` 0.1.0 and made generic. The sandbox's own items in columns, placed by `data-ref`, running across or down, with folding columns, `spacing`, transit-map links in the theme's tones, `highlight` and `reveal`. Every link drawn by default, with pointing fading everything not linked to the item (`links-shown="all"`), or links only along the chain pointed at (`links-shown="chain"`). Each label names the links it labels (`data-links`), and links are redrawn whenever the items move. A `subject` shows only its lineage, within `back` and `on` steps, outlined and scrolled into view, with a trail, Back and `clearTrail()`. Viewers select an item (`sb-select`), follow it (`sb-follow`, cancelable) and go back (`sb-back`); controls inside items stay their own. Viewers pan by dragging and zoom with the buttons, Ctrl or ⌘ and the wheel, or the keys, from the lineage as it is at full size, with `fit()`; or switch it off (`zoom="off"`) to scroll sideways. Full screen (`fullscreen`) lays it over the page, with Escape to leave.
