Goal: gather what belongs together around one question. Say roughly where things go, and the board does the rest.
About

Where it helps

  • Evidence gathered for a question: what the sources say beside what the model does.
  • Comparing things that normally live far apart, such as a passage, a picture and a table row.
  • Grouping a brainstorm or a workshop's findings by theme.

Where something else fits

  • Where things come from and what they lead to: a lineage, whose columns mean something.
  • Things moving through stages: a kanban.
  • One long list you only need to search: a table.

Getting started

Put your content inside it, each with a data-ref, then say roughly where each goes, from 0 to 100 each way. Nothing else is needed.

board.layout = {
  items: [
    { ref: 'GAUGE-2', x: 10, y: 10, group: 'Readings' },
    { ref: 'SRC-4', x: 90, y: 10, group: 'Accounts' }
  ],
  links: [{ from: 'GAUGE-2', to: 'SRC-4', label: 'which is right?' }]
};

Anything wrong is in the console, and in board.problems, so there's no need for a screenshot. Writing a group once saves repeating it: { label: 'Readings', x: 10, y: 10, items: ['GAUGE-2', 'GAUGE-3'] }.

Making it reveal

  • Build each board around one question, and make another board for the next. A few to a few dozen things is plenty.
  • Put things that disagree side by side, so the disagreement is what people see first.
  • Link only what needs saying. A link's words should say why two things are connected, such as "which is right?".
Examples

Why did the river bridge flood?

The readings and accounts disagree about when the water arrived. Each thing is placed in a word or two, and the picture, frames and link are the board's. Drag the background to pan, or press + and − to zoom.

GAUGE-2

Upstream gauge peaked at 03:40.

GAUGE-3

Downstream gauge peaked at 05:10.

SRC-4

Lock keeper: "It was over the deck before three. I've never seen it come up that fast."

SRC-6

Council log: bridge closed at 02:40 "as a precaution".

Q-1

Did a culvert block? It would explain water at the bridge before the upstream peak.

Q-2

Is the photo's clock right? Camera time not yet checked against the log.

Redesigning the 40 bus

A brainstorm with drivers and riders, grouped by theme. Grow an idea with the button and only what's below it moves. The last idea wasn't placed, so the board says so rather than losing it.
