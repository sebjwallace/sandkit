# sb-ontology changelog

## 0.1.0 · 2026-10-18
- First release. Kinds of things and how they relate, as classes, individuals and the relations between them, drawn a few at a time with `show`: each kind's inheritance in its details, the kinds things belong to as keys to light up, the model's problems marked where they are, and boxes viewers can drag. Laid out once, force-directed, and never drifting.
