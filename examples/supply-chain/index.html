Goal: understand end to end how goods get from suppliers to customers, what goes wrong along the way, and what keeps it all running efficiently.

Chapter 1 · The network

One product moves from two suppliers, through a distribution centre (DC), to three stores. Customers buy from the stores between 08:00 and 20:00 . Every location follows one simple rule : when stock plus deliveries on the way drops below a reorder point, order enough to get back up to a target level. Blue dots are trucks on the road.

Try: press Play and watch a few days pass. Click a location to see its stock over time. Then raise demand, or make DC deliveries slower, and see where things break first.
