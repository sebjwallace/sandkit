Chapter 1 · The first seconds after a trip

When the largest unit on the grid trips, 1,320 MW vanish at once and the frequency starts to fall. Everything still spinning slows the fall; batteries answer within two seconds, generators within ten. On windy nights there's less spinning, so the fall is faster, and the frequency can break its 49.5 Hz limit before help arrives. The operators' new screen is built for those nights.

Try: choose a windy night and press play to trip the unit: the frequency breaks the limit. Reset, press Book extra response on the screen, and play again. Then choose Trace in the header's mode picker and click that button to see why it's there.
