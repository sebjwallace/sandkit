# Screen design workshop

18 September 2026. Four control room operators, two engineers, the sandbox team. Notes taken by the sandbox team.

## What the operators asked for

- **The rate of fall.** Everyone agreed: show how fast the frequency is falling, next to the frequency itself, in Hz per second.
- **A warning for low inertia.** On windy nights the fall is faster. Operators want the screen to warn them when inertia is low, before anything trips, not after.
- **One click to book extra response.** Booking more fast response today means a phone call and a form. Operators asked for one click to book extra response from the warning itself.
- **Leave demand disconnection alone.** It's automatic and set in the relays. The screen should say whether it has happened, never offer a button for it.

## Open

- Should the screen predict when the frequency will cross the floor? Some liked it; others worried it would be trusted too much. Not agreed.
