# Frequency planning standard, 2025 edition

Section 3: Holding response. An excerpt, as published by the system operator.

## 3.1 Limits

- In normal operation, frequency is kept between 49.8 Hz and 50.2 Hz.
- After the loss of a generator, frequency must not fall below 49.5 Hz, the statutory limit, except in an emergency.
- Low frequency demand disconnection begins at 48.8 Hz.

## 3.2 How much to hold

Enough response is held at all times to cover the largest single infeed loss on the system. At the time of this edition, that is 1,320 MW, the largest unit connected.

## 3.3 How fast

Fast frequency response must reach its full output within 2 seconds of the frequency falling below 49.8 Hz. Primary response from generators must reach its full output within 10 seconds.

## 3.4 Low inertia

On days of low system inertia, the rate of change of frequency after a loss is higher. The control room may book additional fast response ahead of time when inertia is forecast to be low.
